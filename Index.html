Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What curriculum does the school follow?

We follow the national curriculum framework prescribed by CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education). Our teaching methodology blends traditional values with modern digital learning tools.

Do you provide transportation?

Yes, we provide safe and reliable transportation services covering various routes, with GPS and CCTV on all buses. Parents can track the bus in real-time through our mobile app.

How can I apply for admission?

You can apply online through our website by clicking the "Apply Now" button or visit the school office. Admission for 2025-26 is now open. The process includes form submission, an interaction session, and document verification.

What extracurricular activities are offered?

We offer a wide range of activities including sports (football, basketball, cricket), music, dance, art & craft, debate, robotics, and coding clubs. Students are encouraged to participate in at least one activity per term.
